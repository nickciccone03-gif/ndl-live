The NDL League Bylaws

Overview

The NDL will be governed by the following rules. Any rule changes may be put up for offseason vote by any league manager who is in good-standing. Majority will be required to pass any vote not pertaining to league scoring rules or buy-in amounts, such changes will require 11 approvals with Commissioner abstaining.

Rule changes will not go into effect until the following year.

The Commissioner has sole authority to add, remove, or alter; any by-law, rule, trade, transaction, et al., at his discretion to protect the health and integrity of the league.

Rosters

Each roster consists of a maximum of 30 total players. Rosters will carry over from season to season with 30 total keepers allowed. There are no position minimums but you must have enough players to field a starting lineup prior to the kickoff of any given week. Roster limits for each position are as follows:

Each week owners will start a lineup of 10 players which includes 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 3 FLEX (WR,RB,or TE), and 1 D/ST. No partial lineups are allowed. No players on a bye may be started. Lineups must be submitted before a player's game kicks off in order for that player to be eligible.

In the case that an owner does not set a lineup by 1PM EST on Sunday, the previous week's lineup will be carried over and automatically submitted by the commissioner. If a carried over lineup contains a player on a bye, that player will be replaced with the highest ranked player (Fantasypros rankings) at that position that is currently rostered.

In the event that a manager does not have enough players to field a starting lineup, whether due to injuries, bye weeks, roster construction, or other circumstances; it is the owner’s responsibility to find a replacement and field a full lineup. There will be no exceptions given if there are players available on the waiver wire who can fill the lineup, exceptions may be given if waiver wire is completely bare but this will be an extremely rare occurrence. Please be mindful about bye weeks, position scarcity, and roster construction. If an owner does not make the change on their own, and is not responsive to contact attempts, the commissioner will take control of the franchise and make a good faith effort to correct the issue.

Schedule

The Season will consist of 14 regular season matchups, determined randomly. Each team will play every other team at least once, and three teams twice. Six teams will make the playoffs, with the top two seeds receiving a first round bye.

Standings

Standings are calculated based on the following criteria, in the following order.

  1. Overall winning percentage.
  2. Head-to-head record
  3. Total points scored
  4. All-play winning percentage
  5. Coin flip

If more than two teams are tied in overall winning percentage, the head to head record is determined by highest winning percentage amongst the tied teams in games played against each other.

Example: Team A, B, C are tied with a .667 winning percentage.

TeamGames vs. tied teamsRecord
ABeat B twice, beat C once3-0 (1.000)
BLost to A twice, beat C once1-2 (.333)
CLost to A and B0-2 (.000)

In the above scenario Team A would win the tiebreaker with Team B placed second and Team C third.

All-Play winning percentage is the winning percentage of a team if it had played every team once, every week of the regular season.

Waiver Wire

The NDL uses a Blind Bid/FCFS system to award free agents. All players will be placed on waivers at the time of kickoff of their weekly scheduled game. Owners will be given 100 FABB dollars at the start of the season and are able to place bids on free agents who are on waivers. Bidding will end at 3PM EST each Wednesday and the free agent awarded to the highest bidder. Once bids are processed, all players are removed from waivers and are able to be picked up on a first come first serve basis.

In the event of a blind bidding tie the player will be awarded to the owner who submitted their bid first. All bids must be in increments of 1. 0 bids ARE allowed.

There will be no first come first serve period during the NDL offseason.

Trades

Trades are permitted during the offseason and throughout the regular season up until kickoff of Week 13 of the NFL season. From the kickoff of Week 13 through the conclusion of the championship round in Week 17, no trades are allowed. Owners may trade draft picks up to two years into the future.

A player may not be traded from the moment his weekly game kicks off until the conclusion of that week. If a deal involving such a player is agreed upon during this window, both owners must contact the Commissioner to grant their approval. Once both approvals are received, the trade becomes binding and will be processed by the Commissioner immediately following the conclusion of that week.

Any owner trading THEIR OWN future first- or second-round pick must pay that year's dues in advance before the trade can be executed. For example, if Team A trades its own 2025 pick during the 2024 season, Team A must pay its 2025 dues before the trade is processed. The Treasurer will process the trade once dues are paid.

Required prepayment is as follows:

Own 1st round pickFull dues
Own 2nd round pickHalf dues
Acquired picksNo dues

There are no vetoes, and trades are otherwise processed immediately. However, the Commissioner retains sole discretion and authority to invalidate any trade that jeopardizes the integrity of the league.

Draft

The NDL utilizes an email style, slow draft. The draft consists of four total rounds, each franchise is awarded one pick per round, per season. The draft is a fixed format meaning draft position stays consistent in each round. The player pool will consist of incoming NFL rookies as well as all free agents who are on the waiver wire at the conclusion of week 17.

Draft order

Draft order is determined in the following manner:

Picks 1-10Determined by Potential Points at the conclusion of the regular season, in reverse order (lowest to highest) for the 10 teams that do not play in the finals.
Picks 11 & 12Awarded to the runner-up and champion.

In the event that two teams have the same potential points, the tie breaker goes to the team with the worse regular season record. If that is also a tie it will be broken by a coin flip.

There will be a blind bid waiver period immediately following the draft, and then each week until the conclusion of week three of the NFL preseason. During this time period blind bids will process each Saturday Morning at 7 AM. First come first serve period begins the Monday after week three of the NFL pre-season, immediately after the final blind bids are processed.

Potential Points

Potential points is a scoring system that determines what your point total would have been in any given week had you started all the correct players in each position who posted the highest scores. This point total accumulates each week as the season progresses with the final tally for draft positioning purposes locked in at the conclusion of week 14.

League Dues and Prizes

League dues are $150 per franchise, to be paid to the commissioner prior to kickoff of Week 1 of the NFL Season. If dues are not paid by week six of the regular season that owner will be ineligible for any winnings and winnings will be rolled into the following year's pot. Failure to pay league dues is grounds for immediate removal from the league. Commissioner has discretion to allow payment arrangements for certain circumstances, please reach out to the commissioner if you would like to request an arrangement.

League winner75% of the pot ($1275)
Runner up25% of the pot ($425)

$100 is deducted from the initial pot each year to pay for the league website. Participants of the finals may alter the final payout in whatever way they choose as long as both owners agree in writing prior to kickoff of week 17.

Tanking

Any blatant tanking, (e.g. starting David Blough when Patrick Mahomes is active and on your roster, or Britain Covey over a top 20 WR) will result in your highest, owned (whether it's your own or someone else's) 1ST ROUND draft pick immediately dropping to #12 overall and becoming untradeable. If you do not own a 1st in that year's draft the penalty rolls over to the following year, if that pick is also traded the following year's pick is untradeable and automatically the 12th pick. If you own someone’s pick who violates this rule, it has no effect on the pick.
